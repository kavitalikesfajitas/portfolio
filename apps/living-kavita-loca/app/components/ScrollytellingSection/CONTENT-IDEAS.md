# Scrollytelling Content Ideas for Your Portfolio

## 📖 Story Structure Templates

### Template 1: Classic Hero's Journey
1. **Hook** (0-10%) - Big statement or visual
2. **Background** (10-25%) - Who you are, where you came from
3. **Challenge** (25-40%) - Problem you solve or skills you've developed
4. **Journey** (40-70%) - Your work, projects, experiences
5. **Transformation** (70-85%) - Impact, results, testimonials
6. **Call to Adventure** (85-100%) - Contact CTA

### Template 2: Skills-First Approach
1. **Intro** - Name + tagline
2. **Skill 1** - With example project
3. **Skill 2** - With example project
4. **Skill 3** - With example project
5. **Philosophy** - How you work
6. **CTA** - Let's work together

### Template 3: Project Showcase
1. **Hook** - Attention grabber
2. **Project 1** - Problem → Solution → Impact
3. **Project 2** - Problem → Solution → Impact
4. **Project 3** - Problem → Solution → Impact
5. **About You** - Brief background
6. **CTA** - Contact

## 🎨 Visual Content Ideas

### Backgrounds
- Gradient meshes (subtle, not distracting)
- Your logo watermarked large and faded
- Abstract patterns related to your work
- Dark solid colors with good contrast
- Blurred photos of your workspace

### Slide Images
- **Project screenshots** - Show your best work
- **Process photos** - Sketches, wireframes, iterations
- **Team photos** - Collaborative work
- **Your photo** - Make it personal
- **Icons/illustrations** - Represent concepts
- **Data visualizations** - Show impact with graphs
- **Before/After comparisons** - Show improvements

## ✍️ Copywriting Tips

### Opening Slides
❌ "Hi, I'm a developer"
✅ "I turn ideas into digital experiences"

❌ "Welcome to my portfolio"
✅ "Let me show you what's possible"

### Project Descriptions
Always include:
- **What it is** (1 sentence)
- **The challenge** (What problem?)
- **Your solution** (How you solved it)
- **Impact** (Numbers, results, outcomes)

Example:
"A real-time collaboration tool for remote teams. The challenge: existing tools were too complex. I built a minimal, intuitive interface focused on core features. Result: 40% faster onboarding and 95% user satisfaction."

### Call to Action
❌ "Contact me if interested"
✅ "Let's build something amazing together"

❌ "Check out my resume"
✅ "See my full experience"

## 🎯 Content Positioning by Slide

### Alignment Guidelines
- **Left-aligned** - Reading-heavy content, stories, descriptions
- **Right-aligned** - Alternate for visual rhythm, images
- **Center-aligned** - Big statements, CTAs, quotes, titles

### Top Position Pacing
- 0.05 - Opening (give space at top)
- 0.15-0.85 - Main content (evenly spaced)
- 0.92+ - Closing CTA (leave room at bottom)

## 📊 Content Length Guidelines

- **Titles**: 2-5 words (punchy!)
- **Body text**: 15-40 words per slide
- **Total slides**: 5-10 slides (sweet spot is 6-8)
- **Scroll length**: 5-8 viewport heights

## ⚡ Quick Wins

### Add These for Instant Impact
1. **One impressive stat** - "100K users" or "Featured in TechCrunch"
2. **One client quote** - Social proof
3. **Your best screenshot** - Visual proof of work
4. **Clear CTA** - Make it easy to reach you
5. **Personal touch** - Photo, anecdote, or unique voice

## 🚀 Pro Tips

1. **Start strong** - First slide determines if they keep scrolling
2. **Show, don't tell** - Images > words
3. **One idea per slide** - Don't overcrowd
4. **Create rhythm** - Alternate alignment left/right/center
5. **End with energy** - Strong CTA, not a fade-out
6. **Test on mobile** - Most traffic is mobile
7. **Keep it brief** - Attention span is 3-5 seconds per slide

## 📝 Content Checklist

Before publishing, make sure you have:
- [ ] Clear value proposition in first 2 slides
- [ ] At least 2-3 examples of your work
- [ ] One form of social proof (testimonial, stats, logos)
- [ ] Your unique perspective/voice
- [ ] Obvious next step (CTA)
- [ ] Good visual hierarchy
- [ ] Proper contrast for readability
- [ ] Mobile-friendly text sizes
